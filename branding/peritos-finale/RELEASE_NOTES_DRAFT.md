# SmechOS — Founder Anniversary Edition

Smech marks RC3's close with the Founder Anniversary Edition — a cosmetic
send-off for the Peritos family (est. 2026-06-25 — 2026-10-06): a new
wallpaper, a few hidden surprises, and a thank-you to three releases' worth
of groundwork before SmechOS's own toolchain takes over in Lesobinaska.

Nothing structural changes here — no new RC number, no relation to the
cross-toolchain work landing separately in RC4. Just a proper goodbye to
the family that got SmechOS this far.

Find what's hidden if you can.

---

Happy Birthday, Smech. Three release candidates, a netinst ISO that
actually boots, and a from-scratch build pipeline held together by one
person's own debugging — chasing down boot-loop PAM configs, hunting a
`GLIBC_2.42 cfgetispeed` mismatch to its real root cause instead of
papering over it, and getting a KDE Plasma desktop to run on hardware it
was never handed pre-built for. Peritos was never meant to be perfect —
it was meant to prove SmechOS could exist at all. It did. Here's to
whatever Lesobinaska turns out to be.
