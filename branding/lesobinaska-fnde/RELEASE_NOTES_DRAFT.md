# SmechOS RC4 — Founder Name Day Edition

RC4 opens SmechOS's Lesobinaska family — the first built entirely on
SmechOS's own cross-toolchain rather than the container's inherited
glibc and compiler identity. SABI and SAPI, formerly a dangling comment
pointing at notes that didn't exist, are now real, checked-in documents.
What was once fair to call a sophisticated Ubuntu derivative now stands
on an ABI floor SmechOS built for itself.

That independence didn't come cheap, and Smech isn't pretending it did:
a real x86_64-smechos-linux-gnu toolchain built from source, a from-scratch
glibc, and a cross-compiled LLVM and Clang pulled in along the way once
Mesa's own GPU drivers demanded it. Qt6 and KDE Frameworks ship cross-built
too — not every bug in them is fixed, by design. SmechOS is just getting
started on building a contributor base, and some of what's left open is
left open on purpose.

Named for St. Andrew's Day, Smech's own name day — the night Romanian
tradition says the veil thins and garlic goes up over the door. Fitting,
for a release about SmechOS finally warding off a dependency it had
carried since its very first ISO.

Peritos got SmechOS here. Lesobinaska is SmechOS standing on its own.
